About Kisaan Setu

Farm connection without friction.

We help farmers and buyers connect directly with fair, transparent market access.

Our mission

Remove unnecessary market distance.

Kisaan Setu is designed for a simple idea: crop supply should move from verified farmers to reliable buyers with less uncertainty and fewer intermediaries.

Farmers can list produce, negotiate in-app, and coordinate pick-up. Buyers can discover fresh produce, request terms, and track crop orders.

Operating principles

Trust

Verified profiles and transparent order progress build confidence across the market.

Direct sourcing

Listings and inquiries reduce the gap between farms and buyers.

Fair pricing

Open negotiation allows price discovery before order confirmation.
